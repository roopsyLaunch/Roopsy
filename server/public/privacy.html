Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 06, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all of our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and profile details provided during account creation.
  • Location Information: Precise or approximate location data from your mobile device to show you nearby barbers, beauty parlors, and tailors. This is optional but highly recommended.
  • Booking & Activity Details: Your appointment history, services booked, and shop preferences.
  • Device & Log Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash logs.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate appointment booking, scheduling, and notifications.
  • To help you locate nearby beauty parlor, barber, and tailoring services.
  • To improve and optimize the performance and design of the Roopsy application.
  • To communicate updates, notifications, and customer support messages.

3. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following scenarios:

  • With Service Providers: Sharing name, phone, and booking info with the specific partner (barber, salon, or tailor) you make a booking with.
  • For Legal Purposes: If required by law, regulation, or legal process to protect the rights and safety of Roopsy or its users.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You have full control over your data, including the right to request account deletion.

Account Deletion & Data Removal

If you wish to delete your account and erase all personal information associated with it, you can do so directly in the App under Settings > Account > Delete Account, or submit a request via email to support@roopsy.com. We will process and delete your data within 7 business days.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at:
